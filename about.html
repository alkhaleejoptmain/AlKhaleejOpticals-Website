Our Story

Providing Full Range of Vision Care in RAK

From our first store in Ras Al Khaimah to becoming a trusted name in eye care, Al Khaleej Opticals has remained committed to personalized service, modern diagnostics, and premium eyewear.

Today we serve customers from three branches in Ras Al Khaimah, including two in Al Nakheel, and partner with companies through our corporate eye care programmes.

We combine expert optometry with a warm, welcoming experience — helping every customer see better and feel confident in their choice of frames and lenses.
