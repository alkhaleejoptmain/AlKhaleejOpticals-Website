Supporting Your Workforce with Better Vision

At Al Khaleej Opticals, we understand that healthy vision plays a critical role in workplace productivity, safety, and employee well-being. Our Corporate Vision Care Program is designed to help organizations provide professional eye care services and premium eyewear solutions to their employees.

Whether your workforce spends long hours in front of digital screens, operates machinery, drives vehicles, or works in demanding environments, our team provides comprehensive eye care services tailored to your organization's needs.

Corporate FAQs

Corporate Eye Care Tie-Ups — Your Questions

Do you offer corporate eye care programmes in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. Our Corporate Vision Care Programme covers employee eye examinations, prescription eyewear, digital eye strain solutions, safety eyewear and contact lenses for companies of every size across Ras Al Khaimah and the UAE.

How can my company tie up with Al Khaleej Opticals?

Send us a short enquiry through the corporate proposal form or WhatsApp +971 56 774 3136. We will understand your needs, prepare a customised proposal, organise employee enrolment and provide the eye care services and ongoing support.

Which industries do you support?

We support corporate offices, healthcare, education, retail and hospitality, construction and engineering, and transportation and logistics teams.

Do you handle bulk orders and delivery for companies?

Yes. We offer streamlined bulk delivery for corporate and bulk orders, with timely execution and after-sales adjustment support.

Can employees use their insurance together with a corporate programme?

In many cases, yes. We work with major insurers and can structure a programme around your team's existing insurance. Tell us your insurer when you enquire.

Which insurance companies do you work with?

We work with ADNIC, NAS, Nextcare, Mednet, Orient and Saico Health. Coverage depends on your individual plan, so please bring your insurance card or call us before your visit to confirm.
