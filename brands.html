Eyewear Brands — Quick Answers

Where is Al Khaleej Opticals located in Ras Al Khaimah?

Al Khaleej Opticals has 3 branches in Ras Al Khaimah, including two in Al Nakheel. Open our Contact page for Google Maps directions to each branch, or call or WhatsApp +971 56 774 3136.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Saturday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and on Friday from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

Which eyewear brands do you stock?

Our brands include Ray-Ban, Emporio Armani, Armani Exchange, EGMA, Vincci, Ocelot, Lyrica, GKB, Matrix and Pixel, plus Essilor and Kodak Lens for prescription lenses. Availability changes, so ask our team about current styles.
