FAQs

AI Frame Finder — Your Questions

How does the AI Frame Finder work?

Upload a selfie or use your camera on our AI Frame Finder page. The tool maps your facial landmarks in your browser to estimate your face shape and suggests frame styles that suit it, with a virtual try-on.

Are my photos uploaded or stored?

No. The AI Frame Finder analyses your photo in your browser. Your selfie is not uploaded to our servers or stored by us.

What lenses help with long hours on computers and phones?

Blue-light filtering and anti-reflective lenses can reduce discomfort during long screen sessions. Our optometrists will recommend the right option based on your prescription and how you work.
