Where can I buy Kodak Lens lenses in Ras Al Khaimah?

Al Khaleej Opticals sells Kodak Lens lenses at our Al Nakheel branches in Ras Al Khaimah. Call or WhatsApp +971 56 774 3136 to check current options before you visit.

Can I get Kodak Lens lenses in the frame I already own?

In many cases yes. Bring your frame and prescription and our optometrists will check whether the lens can be fitted and what coatings suit you.

Cover depends on your individual insurance plan. We work with ADNIC, NAS, Nextcare, Mednet, Orient and Saico Health, so bring your card or call us and we will help you check your benefits.

Can my company order Kodak Lens lenses for employees?

Yes. Our Corporate Vision Care Programme supports companies across Ras Al Khaimah and the UAE with eye tests, prescription eyewear and bulk delivery. Visit our corporate page or message us on WhatsApp to start.
